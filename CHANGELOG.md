# Changelog

All notable changes to Tenrec. Download the latest version at [tenrec.app](https://tenrec.app).

## 1.0.0 · November 2026

The first public release.

- Hear friends typing, faintly, in stereo, each in their own seat around you
- Circles with a six character code and an invite link
- Six keyboard sounds: Soft, Creamy, Thock, Tactile, Clicky and Topre
- Rooms: Library, Café, Cabin and Lo-fi
- Your own tenrec with six colors and hats that looks around, blinks and bounces
- 25 minute sprints together
- Rock paper scissors to pick the room
- Taps and "I'm starting" nudges
- Open desk to sit with strangers, shown only by a country flag
- Together log with today, this week and a streak
- Quieter on calls when your microphone is in use
- Automatic or manual mode
- Signed and notarized by Apple
