## Working alone gets too quiet

Long days, an empty room, just you and the keyboard. Tenrec sits in your menu bar and lets you hear a friend typing, faintly, from wherever they are. No video. No chat. Just the quiet sound of someone else getting work done, which makes it a lot easier to keep going yourself.

It's body doubling for your Mac.

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/screens.jpg?v=2" alt="Tenrec onboarding, menu bar panel and settings" width="100%">

## How it works

1. **Download and open it.** Tenrec lives in your menu bar. There's no account to make.
2. **Make a circle.** You get a six character code and a link like `tenrec.app/i/59J8BB`.
3. **Send it to a friend.** When they join, you hear each other type. That's it.

## What's inside

| | |
|---|---|
| 🎧 **Friends, in stereo** | Each friend sits in their own spot around you, so you can tell who's typing. |
| ⌨️ **Six keyboard sounds** | Soft, Creamy, Thock, Tactile, Clicky and Topre. Pick how your friends sound to you. |
| 🌧️ **Rooms** | Library, Café, Cabin and Lo-fi ambience that plays while you type or friends are here. |
| 🦔 **Your own tenrec** | Pick a color and a hat. Your tenrec looks around, blinks and bounces when friends type. |
| 🏃 **Sprints** | Start a 25 minute sprint together and finish it as a group. |
| ✊ **Play for the room** | A quick rock paper scissors. The winner picks the room for everyone. |
| 👋 **Taps and nudges** | Tap a friend to say hi, or nudge them when it's time to start. |
| 🌍 **Open desk** | No friends online? Sit with strangers. No names, only a country flag. |
| 📈 **Together log** | See how long you worked together today and this week, with a streak. |
| 🔇 **Quieter on calls** | Friends fade down while your microphone is in use. |
| ✍️ **Automatic or manual** | Tenrec notices when you type, or you flip "I'm working" yourself. |

## Privacy

Tenrec was built to share as little as possible.

- **It never knows which keys you press.** It only notices *that* a key went down. Friends receive the timing, never the letters.
- **No account and no analytics.** Your identity is a random ID kept in your Mac's Keychain.
- **Friends see only what you choose:** your display name, your color, and your taps.
- **The relay forgets.** It only remembers that a circle code exists, and drops it after 30 days without use.

Read the full [privacy policy](https://tenrec.app/privacy.html) and [terms](https://tenrec.app/terms.html).

## Permissions

Automatic mode asks for **Input Monitoring**, so Tenrec can notice a key was pressed. It uses a listen only event tap and never reads characters. Prefer not to grant it? Choose manual mode and Tenrec sends a calm typing rhythm while you're working.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later
- An internet connection to hear your circle

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is it really free?</b></summary>
<br>Yes. No trial, no subscription, no account.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can my friends see what I'm typing?</b></summary>
<br>No. Tenrec only sends when keys are pressed, not which ones. There's nothing to read.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do we need to talk or turn on a camera?</b></summary>
<br>No. That's the point. You just hear each other working.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How many people can be in a circle?</b></summary>
<br>Tenrec is made for small circles of friends, coworkers or study buddies.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where is the source code?</b></summary>
<br>This repository is the home for Tenrec's releases, updates and feedback. The source isn't public.
